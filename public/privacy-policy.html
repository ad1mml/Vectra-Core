Vectra Core

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

What we collect

When you use VectraCore, we collect the following information:

  • The email address you provide to unlock beta access and verify your account.
  • Confirmation that your email was verified via a one-time code.
  • Chart images you upload for analysis.
  • The questions you ask and the analysis history tied to your account.
  • Feedback, support messages, and form submissions you send us.
  • Device and browser data such as IP address, browser type, and basic usage logs for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

We use your data to provide VectraCore, keep your workspace connected across visits, generate chart analysis, respond to support requests, improve the product, and maintain security. Your email and analysis history allow us to keep past chart analyses and conversations available when you return.

We do not sell your personal data.

Marketing emails

If you agree to receive them during sign-up, we may send you product updates, new features, discounts, and other promotional emails in addition to account, security, and verification emails needed to operate the service.

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in those emails or by contacting us at privacy@vectracore.app. Opting out of marketing emails does not affect account, security, or verification emails.

Third-party processing

To generate analysis, chart images and questions you submit are sent to Google's Gemini AI models for processing. Market news headlines used in answers are sourced from Finnhub.

We may also use service providers for hosting, storage, analytics, email delivery, and security. These providers process data only to help us run the service.

We do not use your data to train third-party AI models, and our service providers do not build advertising profiles on you from your VectraCore inputs.

Local storage

Your email may be stored in your browser's local storage so you do not have to re-enter it each visit. Clearing your browser data will remove it. If you use a shared device, you should clear local storage when finished.

Data retention

We retain analysis history so you can review past sessions. We keep account data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward if needed for legal, security, or operational reasons. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

Deleted data may remain temporarily in backups until those backups are rotated.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. This includes encryption in transit where available and access controls on stored data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your own, including by our service providers. By using VectraCore, you understand that your data may be transferred and stored in those locations.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. You may also opt out of marketing emails at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

VectraCore is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and may notify you by email or through the service.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at privacy@vectracore.app or use our Contact Us page.

This policy is written to reflect the current VectraCore product as described here. You should have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before launch or if your data practices change.
