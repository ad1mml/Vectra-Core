Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

What we collect

When you use VectraCore, we collect the information needed to provide and improve the service.

  • The email address you provide to access the beta and verify your account.
  • Confirmation that your email was verified with a one-time code.
  • Chart images you upload for analysis.
  • The questions you ask and the analysis history associated with your account.
  • Feedback and support messages you send through our forms.

How we use it

We use your information to operate VectraCore, generate chart analysis, keep your workspace synced across visits, respond to support requests, and improve the product. Your email and analysis history help us keep your past sessions available when you return.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Marketing emails

If you agree to all policies during sign-up, you agree that VectraCore may send you product updates, new features, discounts, and other promotional emails, in addition to essential account, security, and verification emails.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails, or by contacting us through our Contact Us page and asking to be removed. This will not affect account, security, or verification emails that we may still need to send.

Third-party processing

To generate analysis, chart images and questions you submit are sent to Google's Gemini AI models for processing. Market news headlines shown in answers are sourced from Finnhub. These providers process your request and return the result; they are not used to build advertising profiles on you.

Local storage

Your email is stored in your browser's local storage so you do not have to enter it again on each visit. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Data retention

We retain your analysis history so you can review past sessions. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please send them through our Contact Us page.

VectraCore is currently in free public beta. This policy may be updated as the product evolves. It is provided as a general template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
