About VectraCore

VectraCore is an analytical workspace built for anyone who looks at charts — whether you're just starting out or you've been reading structure and liquidity for years.

The idea is simple: take a screenshot of any chart, upload it, and get a clear, structured read. No complicated setup. No need to already be an expert. Just the chart and a direct answer.

What we built

Most traders move between a charting platform, a news feed, and their own notes. VectraCore brings structural analysis, liquidity mapping, multi-timeframe context, and live market headlines into one conversational workspace — so you can ask about a setup or the market and get a grounded response in one place.

How the analysis works

Every chart is read in layers, in this order:

1. Structure

Trend direction, swing highs and lows, and key levels — the frame everything else is checked against.

2. Liquidity mapping

Where stops and orders are likely resting: order blocks, fair value gaps, equal highs and lows.

3. Multi-timeframe context

The same setup checked against higher and lower timeframes, rather than read in isolation.

4. Macro awareness

Live headlines and known events folded in, so a technical read isn't blind to the wider market.

Which layers appear in a chart read depends on your plan. Default and Pro chart analyses are technical. VIP adds true multi-timeframe context and brings fundamental and macro information into the same read.

When the layers don't line up into a clean setup, VectraCore says so. WAIT is a valid and common outcome. "Structure-first" and "liquidity-aware" aren't slogans — they describe the order the analysis actually runs in.

Who it's for

VectraCore is for anyone who can take a screenshot of a chart and wants a clear second opinion — beginners learning to read structure, intermediate traders refining their process, and experienced traders who want a fast, consistent analytical layer.
