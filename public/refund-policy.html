Refund Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

No refunds

All VectraCore subscription payments are non-refundable. Once a subscription payment has been successfully processed, we do not provide refunds, credits, or prorated reimbursements for unused time or unused features, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

Introductory pricing

VectraCore offers introductory pricing so you can evaluate the service before the standard subscription price applies. Pro is offered at $19.99 per month for the first two billing cycles before renewing at $99 per month. VIP is offered at $49.99 per month for the first two billing cycles before renewing at $219.99 per month.

These introductory periods are intended to give you enough time to test VectraCore and decide whether the service is suitable for you. Subscribing means you accept the applicable introductory pricing, renewal price, and our no-refund policy.

Before you subscribe

Please review the plan, billing frequency, introductory pricing, renewal price, and features shown on the pricing page before completing your subscription. If the service does not meet your expectations after you have had the opportunity to use it, that does not create an entitlement to a refund.

Cancellation

You may cancel a recurring subscription to prevent future renewals. Cancellation does not refund a payment that has already been processed, and access remains subject to the applicable subscription and account terms.

Billing errors

If you believe you were charged because of a technical or billing error, contact us through our Contact Us page with the relevant billing details so we can investigate the issue.

This policy describes VectraCore's intended refund terms and is not legal advice. Consumer-protection laws in some jurisdictions may provide rights that cannot be waived by a contract. Where applicable law requires a refund or other remedy, those legal requirements will apply.
