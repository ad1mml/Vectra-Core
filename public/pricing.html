FAQ

Common questions

Why is there a limit on chart analyses instead of unlimited for everyone?

Each analysis runs a full structural read — trend, liquidity, multi-timeframe context. That takes real compute. The limits keep the Default and Pro tiers priced low while still giving you a genuine, full-depth read on every chart you submit — not a stripped-down version.

What's the actual difference between Pro and VIP?

Pro gives you more monthly analyses and lets you ask fundamental and geopolitical questions in chat, but each chart analysis stays technical-only. VIP combines technical and fundamental analysis in the read itself, adds multi-timeframe context, an economic calendar, and chart comparison over time — for traders who want the full picture in one pass.

Is the Default plan actually usable, or just a teaser?

It's a real test of the tool: 20 full chart analyses a month, unlimited chat, no card required. Enough to see how VectraCore reasons through a handful of your own setups before deciding if you need more volume.

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your billing settings at any time — changes apply from your next billing cycle.
