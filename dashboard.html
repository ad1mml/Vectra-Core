HOW TO READ IT

From logged prediction to verified result

Every prediction is checked against real candles. The dashboard only counts a trade once its stop loss or take profit was actually hit.

1

Prediction logged

The app saves each decision the moment it is made. It shows here as awaiting.

2

Checked automatically

New and open trades are compared with real Twelve Data candles on a schedule.

3

Win, loss or pending

Take profit hit first is a win, stop loss first is a loss, neither yet is pending.

4

Grouped by symbol

Win rate, average R and total R are broken down by symbol, side, timeframe and plan.

5

Trust the sample size

Hover a win-rate bar for its 95% range. Aim for 30+ completed trades per row.
